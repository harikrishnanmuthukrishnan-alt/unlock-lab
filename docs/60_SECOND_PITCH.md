# UNLOCK Lab — 60-second pitch

> There is a printer sitting idle in the lab. A student needs it for a project, but cannot access it safely or fairly. Ordinary calendar bookings do not solve the problem: training, approval, custody, disputes and fairness matter.
>
> UNLOCK Lab turns idle college resources into accountable opportunities. Students discover a suitable resource, verify eligibility through a human-reviewed Skill Passport, and request a slot. Our transparent FairQueue reduces monopolisation, while owners retain approval authority. A documented handover records equipment condition, and every return, delay and incident remains auditable.
>
> This is a working end-to-end product. Watch us submit a booking, approve it, check in, record condition, return the resource, confirm custody and open the complete audit history. Our approach combines access, safety, fairness and accountability without delegating important decisions to AI.

## Judge demo order

1. Student searches 3D printer and submits request.
2. Owner reviews eligibility and priority, then approves.
3. Student checks in, records condition and returns.
4. Owner confirms receipt; both inspect the audit trail.
5. Show a competing request/waitlist and training review.

## Distinct value

**“Other booking systems ask whether a resource is free. UNLOCK Lab asks whether the *right person* can use it *safely, fairly, and accountably*.”**

## If asked why no mandatory AI

The core resource access path intentionally uses transparent, deterministic rules and human approval for safety. The optional LabMate AI assistant helps discover resources and explain policy without authority to grant access or assign penalties.
